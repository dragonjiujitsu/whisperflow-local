whisperflow-local for macOS

A local Apple Silicon dictation pipeline: hotkey, microphone capture, MLX speech-to-text, Unsloth cleanup, and focus-checked insertion into the frontmost app.

HotkeyCmd+Shift+Space through pynput toggles recording.
Capturesounddevice records 16 kHz mono float32 audio and feeds overlay RMS.
Speech-to-textlightning-whisper-mlx runs distil-large-v3 locally on Apple Silicon.
CleanupUnsloth Studio serves unsloth/Qwen3.5-4B-MTP-GGUF through a localhost OpenAI-compatible API, with direct Unsloth CLI fallback.
InsertThe frontmost process is re-checked before paste; insertion uses pbcopy, pbpaste, and Cmd+V.
AutostartA user LaunchAgent starts the app at login.
